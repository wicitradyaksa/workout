Full Body · Dumbbell + Bench

3 days a week · about 45–50 min
Easy week
Every 6th week: 1 set, about 60% weight

Main workout

Plus (optional): a 15–20 min brisk walk later today, e.g. after a meal. It helps you reach about 150 walking minutes a week.

Rules

Weekly schedule
  • Mon / Wed / Fri: workout, plus an optional 15–20 min walk.
  • Tue / Thu: brisk walk, 45–60 min. Pace: you can talk, but not sing.
  • Sat: sprints (6–8 × 50 m) and a 15 min walk. Saturday keeps a rest day before the next workout.
  • Sun: full rest. Normal daily steps only.
  • Aim for about 8,000+ steps a day. Tap any day at the top to see or log it.
The one rule
  • Work inside the rep range. Hit the top of the range on all sets with clean form → tap Next weight, then start again at the bottom of the range.
  • Not at the top yet → same weight, try to add 1 rep somewhere.
  • Reps go down 3 sessions in a row → tap Step down, then climb again.
  • Only 2 sets, so both must be real work: end each set with 1–2 reps left in the tank.
How to run it
  1. Week 1: find your level. Easily past the top of the range → step up. Can't reach the bottom on both sets → step down.
  2. Warm up 5 min, then 1–2 easy practice sets of each main lift (they don't count).
  3. Rest about 2 min between sets. Split squat: 2 sets on each leg, weaker leg first.
  4. Calf raises: one set per leg during bench rest, one during overhead-press rest.
  5. Lift stalls 3–4 weeks while sleeping and eating well → add a 3rd set to that lift only.
  6. Optional 4th session after about 3 months if you recover well. Keep a rest day between most sessions.
  7. Safety: don't grind a failing press. Lower the dumbbells to your thighs, sit up, put them down.
  8. Protein 1.6–2 g per kg bodyweight, sleep 7–9 hours.
